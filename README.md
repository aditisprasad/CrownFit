# 👑 CrownFit

### AI-Powered Pageant Preparation & Performance Platform

CrownFit is a full-stack AI platform designed to help aspiring pageant contestants **discover opportunities, analyze their presentation, practice interviews, receive personalized coaching, and track their preparation journey** in one place.

> **Assess → Practice → Improve → Reassess**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 Anaira — AI Pageant Coach
Personalized AI coaching based on the user's preparation data, performance, confidence check-ins, and progress.

### 👑 Pageant Discovery
Discover and filter pageant opportunities by:
- Country
- State/Region
- City
- Age
- Registration status

Supports verified information, official links, bookmarks, and transparent source/empty states without fabricated data.

### 📍 Professionals Discovery
Real-world professional discovery powered by **Google Places API (New)**.

Find:
- Pageant Coaches
- Modeling/Fashion Institutes
- Makeup Artists
- Fashion Designers
- Photographers
- Fitness & Wellness providers

Displays real Google information such as ratings, addresses, phone numbers, websites and Maps links.

### ✨ Skin & Presentation Analyzer
AI-powered visual analysis of:
- Blemishes
- Redness
- Uneven tone
- Texture
- Shine
- Dryness appearance
- Under-eye appearance

Includes confidence/preparation check-ins and personalized pageant-preparation guidance.

> Visual skin assessment — not a medical diagnosis.

### 🧍 Posture & Stage Presence
Browser-based computer vision using **MediaPipe** to analyze measurable posture signals such as:
- Head alignment
- Shoulder alignment
- Hip alignment
- Torso inclination
- Symmetry
- Stability

### 🎤 Mock Jury
Practice pageant interview questions and improve communication, answer structure and stage confidence.

### 📅 Preparation Plan
Organize and track pageant preparation across interviews, presentation, fitness, portfolio and personal development.

### 📊 Digital Twin & Insights
A personalized view of the contestant's preparation journey using real saved data and performance signals.

---

## 🧠 Product Vision

CrownFit is designed to evolve beyond a collection of AI features into a **personalized pageant preparation ecosystem**.

```text
        Analyze
           ↓
   Identify Weaknesses
           ↓
        Practice
           ↓
     AI Coaching
           ↓
      Reassess
           ↓
    Track Progress
````

The long-term goal is a **Crown Readiness Engine** that combines computer vision, AI coaching, interview performance and preparation data to generate personalized recommendations.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

CrownFit follows a **no fabricated data** principle.

* User data is isolated through authentication and RLS.
* Personal analyses are private.
* Saved images are stored privately.
* API keys remain server-side.
* Missing information is clearly identified instead of being invented.
* AI features do not make medical or psychological diagnoses.
